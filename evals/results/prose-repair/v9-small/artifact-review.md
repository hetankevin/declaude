# Reading the actual drafts

This is the coordinator's manual inspection, not an independent human label. The three cases were deliberately selected from known development failures, so neither the selection nor this inspection is blinded.

## Argument progression

In `large-dev-a-12`, the previous v8 draft combined examples, policy, missing readings and counts in one dense paragraph. V9 gives the distinction between equipment failures and unusual results its own paragraph, explicitly saying these are examples of the unchanged rule. The next paragraph groups the table fields, runs with no usable readings, and the attempted/retained counts. This is an improvement over that particular v8 defect, but the no-skill draft still gives the missing-reading problem more room and separates the reporting burden from the limits of transparency. The change does not establish that v9 has the better overall argument.

The museum case, `large-dev-b-09`, is already strong without a skill. V9 retains the main recommendation, original object's historical value, quoted costs, untested visitor benefit and the committee's alternative decision. It makes the resource implication explicit: the lower quoted cost leaves more resources for the trial. The no-skill draft already does this too. No decisive superiority to that baseline is apparent from inspection.

## Proof exposition

In `large-dev-b-14`, v9 preserves the fixed-population setup, uniform pair/index sampling, conditional expectation, zero mixed term, exact variance and both degeneracies. It does not introduce independence. Before evaluating the pairwise sum it writes the residual second moment as the average of those squared differences. It then explains why ordered pairs count each unordered pair twice and displays the intermediate centering identity. This addresses the purpose-before-calculation weakness observed in an earlier v8 proof.

The mathematical argument remains valid, including at N=2 and zero population variance. This checks one proof only. Proof and research-voice reference files being unchanged does not prove that other proof outputs cannot regress under the new core.

## What was not tested

The full skill bundle was supplied, matching the old isolated-rewrite benchmark. The candidate's recommendation to load references selectively was not tested here. The persistent file-editing experiment stopped before any matched pair. No long-horizon or fresh-holdout performance is established. A release decision must retain these limits and the actual blind comparisons, including any unfavorable judgment.
