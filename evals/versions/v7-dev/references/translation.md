# Translate rhetorical prose into plain English

Recover the meaning behind inflated, repetitive, or overly abstract phrasing. Rewrite the relationship in ordinary English. A paragraph saying four times that a cover keeps dust out can become one sentence. A paragraph explaining a mechanism may need every step. The amount of compression follows the content and the reader's needs.

Preserve every distinct fact, instruction, condition, permission, prohibition, preference, comparison, uncertainty, temporal relationship, and causal or logical dependency. Keep the speech act: describing what happened is not directing what should happen. Do not turn a possibility into a fact, a prerequisite into sufficiency, or a trigger into an exclusivity rule. Preserve names, quotations, commands, code, and technical terminology whose wording must remain fixed.

Remove announcements such as “the honest verdict,” unsupported claims of importance, and closing slogans when they add nothing. Replace a decorative metaphor or dense compound with the supported relationship, not a new interpretation. “Approval-gated publication” says approval is required; it does not establish who approves or whether approval alone is sufficient. Keep a contrast if it rules out a plausible misunderstanding. Keep literal engineering meanings and established technical terms.

A repeated claim can be redundant, but explanation and navigation do useful work too. A reminder can connect evidence to a conclusion, and a short signpost can help readers find the next step. Judge these by their role in the complete passage. Do not delete them simply because their propositions are inferable elsewhere.

Keep deliberate humor, voice, and effective organization. Revise whole sentences or passages where needed, without making a polished document look newly rewritten for its own sake. The result should be direct, idiomatic, and easy to use, not merely shorter.

Compare the result with the source for omissions, strengthened claims, lost qualifications, altered timing, and changed dependencies. If wording is ambiguous, do not supply an unsupported referent or explanation. When the user explicitly excludes a discussion from the revision, preserve the meaning of the retained material without reintroducing the removed content.

Return the requested text, without an editing preface or verdict, unless commentary was requested.
